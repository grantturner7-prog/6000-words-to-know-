## 4. The stories

### Beginner, part 1: A Cold Morning Climb

Sam and Amy go up a **mountain**. The sun comes up in the **east**. It is **only** six o'clock. Amy is **able** to walk fast. Their teacher is with them. Soon the weather turns **bad**. Rain falls. The wind is strong. Sam may **lose** his hat. He holds it. Amy has a **towel** in her bag. Sam dries his face. Amy says, “**Remember** to bring a coat.” At the top, they type a note. They save it in a **file**. They go home at four **pm**.

### Beginner, part 2: The Old Tree

Today is a **holiday**. Tom goes to see his grandmother. She was **born** in a small town. The town is in the **west**. Tom must **cross** a busy road. He looks both ways. Tom is late. “I am **sorry**,” he says. Grandmother has a big tree. Tom has a **chance** to pick the fruit. He can **shake** the tree to get it. Grandmother has a small tree too. A cold night can **kill** it. She likes to **teach** Tom about plants. At night, she tells a sea **god** story.

### Intermediate, part 1: The Morning Climb

Last Saturday, our class climbed a high **mountain** near the city. It was **only** five o'clock when we started. At the top, we watched the sun come up in the **east**. Amy was **able** to run up the last hill. She waited for us there. Then the weather turned **bad**, and cold rain fell on our heads. Tom gave me a **towel** to dry my hair. “**Remember** to wear your coat next time,” he said. I was afraid I would **lose** my hat in the wind. So I held it with both hands. On the way down, Sam wrote many notes. He saved them in a **file** on his phone. We reached the school bus at four **pm**, tired and happy.

### Intermediate, part 2: The Farmer and the Sea

During the winter **holiday**, my grandfather told us an old story. He was **born** in a small village. The village was in the **west**. In the story, a farmer loved his plants. The cold winter could **kill** all of them. So he asked the sea **goddess** for help. He asked many times. “I am **sorry**,” he said. She listened and gave him a **chance** to find a warm land. The farmer had to **cross** the sea in a small boat. The strong wind made the boat **shake**. He stayed calm. In the end, she **taught** him how to grow plants there.

### Advanced, part 1: A Long Walk to the Top

My cousin Ken, who lives near a high **mountain**, invited us for a weekend walk. We left home very early because Ken wanted us to watch the sun come up. The sun comes up in the **east**, so we climbed the path on that side. The path was long, but everyone was **able** to climb it, including my grandfather. When we reached the top, the sky was still dark and it was **only** five o'clock. Ken said that the weather can turn **bad** quickly. He asked us to **remember** to bring warm jackets next time. On the way down, rain began to fall, so Ken gave each of us a clean **towel**. I was afraid that I might **lose** my notebook, so I kept it in my pocket. Later, I wrote about the trip and saved it in a **file**. I sent it to my friends. We got back to the village at six **pm**, tired but proud.

### Advanced, part 2: Summer in the Old Town

During the summer **holiday**, my family visited my grandmother and grandfather. Their small town is in the **west** of the country. My grandfather was **born** in that town, and he knows every street and every old story. In the kitchen, he wanted to **teach** me how to make noodle soup. Cooking with him was fun, but I dropped a cup on the floor. I said I was **sorry** for the mess. My grandmother laughed and told me an old story about a sea **god** who helped a kind farmer. The farmer had a **chance** to start a new life, and he took it. The next day, we went to a market by a long river. To reach the market, we had to **cross** an old bridge. As we walked, the wind made it **shake** a little, and my father held our hands. At the market, a seller said that hot weather had **killed** many plants. So the vegetables were expensive.
